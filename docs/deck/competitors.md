## What each one lacks, and why Fammy is better for this job

- **PCIT clinic.** The gold standard: a therapist codes every sentence and coaches live. It needs your child in
  the room, 12–20 weekly sessions and, where there are waitlists, about three months of waiting. Fammy gives the
  practice step tonight, with the same targets.
- **Happypillar.** Scored your play against PCIT skills, but only with your child present, and since Manatee bought
  it in October 2025 it is not sold to parents. Fammy is the step before: practice with a simulated child.
- **Vipo.AI.** Practice with an "AI child" and feedback, but a Boston University research project with no product
  or price we could find.
- **ChildMind AI.** You can chat with a virtual child, but nothing scores your sentences against clinic numbers, so
  you never learn whether you hit the skill.
- **Good Inside.** Proof that parents pay (about $34M revenue in 2025, 100K+ subscribers): videos, scripts and a
  chatbot that tell you what to say. Nothing checks that you can say it.
- **ChatGPT.** Free and will play a child when asked, but it keeps no score, and the same answer can be judged
  differently every time. Fammy's rules give the same score for the same sentence, and you can dispute it.

## What not to claim

Not "nobody does this": ChildMind AI and Vipo.AI have a virtual child, Happypillar and clinics score. The new part
is the combination: scored practice against the clinic targets, without the child, in three minutes.
